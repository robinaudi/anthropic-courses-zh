## Wrap-up

> https://anthropic.skilljar.com/enterprise-train-the-trainer/336780

## Wrap-up

Objective: Complete your trainer journey by understanding available support systems, how to implement best practices for team enablement, and gain the troubleshooting knowledge needed to confidently guide colleagues through their AI integration process.

Top Tips for Enabling Teams on Claude

Start with the “Why” before the “How”

Emphasize that Claude enhances human expertise rather than substituting for it. Help teams understand that they remain the decision-makers and domain experts, while Claude provides processing power and speed. Connect Claude's capabilities directly to their specific pain points and workflows.

Prioritize Live Demos with Real Work Scenarios

Dedicate significant session time to hands-on demonstrations using actual examples. Show Claude solving real problems they face. Have participants follow along in their own Claude instances during demos, building muscle memory through practice. Focus on tactical, immediately applicable use cases rather than abstract possibilities, demonstrating how Claude fits into their existing workflows and tools.

Teach Progressive Prompting Through Their Work

Demonstrate prompt evolution using actual workflows. Show three levels with the same task: Basic prompt (mediocre results) → Add context and constraints (better output) → Fully structured with role, context, examples, and format (excellent results). Using a real use cases makes improvement tangible. Teach the meta-technique: having Claude write better prompts by asking "What prompt would have gotten me this exact result from the beginning?"

Leverage Projects and Knowledge Bases for Team Context

Show teams how to create dedicated Projects with relevant documents, custom instructions, and team-specific knowledge bases. This allows Claude to understand organizational context, terminology, and workflows. Teams can upload extensive documentation (100+ transcripts, 15+ reports) to create a truly customized AI assistant that speaks their language and understands their work. Show how to set granular project permissions - emphasize that project chats are private by default.

Set Realistic Expectations with the J-Curve

Prepare teams for the inevitable "period of disruption" where productivity may temporarily dip as they learn new skills. Acknowledge that initial prompts might not work perfectly and that iteration is part of the learning process. Emphasize that every expert user went through this valley, and encourage patience and experimentation. Frame prompting issues as learning opportunities that build expertise.

Create Function-Specific Champions and Use Cases

Develop tailored examples and tutorials for each functional area. Identify and train champions within each department who can demonstrate relevant use cases and provide peer support. Encourage teams to discuss and share their discoveries with colleagues, creating a culture of continuous learning and innovation. Provide role-specific resources and maintain an accessible repository of tutorials and best-in-class projects & prompts organized by feature and function.

### Get Help From Anthropic's Customer Support Team

When to contact Anthropic customer support

Our customer support team is dedicated to helping you have the best possible experience with Claude.ai. They are here to support you in resolving technical issues, answer your questions about features, and ensure you can use Claude effectively for your needs!

Where our support team can help:

- Technical issues within Claude - When you're experiencing bugs, errors, login problems, or the Claude website, mobile app, or desktop app isn't working properly.
- Feature functionality questions - When you're unsure how to use specific features like artifacts, file uploads, or how to perform certain actions within Claude.
- Access or permission problems - If you're unable to access features that should be available to you, or experiencing issues with project or team collaboration.
- Performance or response quality concerns - When Claude is providing unexpected outputs or experiencing unusual delays.
To get help, navigate to Claude.ai > Click the messenger icon in the bottom left corner > select "Get Help" >Search our help center documentation first. If you can’t find the answer, click “Send us a message”
